**App details** [446/500]:
Storevine Bundles adds a bundle block to your product pages. Offer products bought together with a variant picker for each item, quantity breaks like buy 3 and save 15%, or mix and match across a set of products. Discounts apply automatically at checkout through a Shopify discount, so there are no duplicate products or price edits. See orders and revenue from each bundle. The price is flat: there's no cap on how much you sell through bundles.
