**App name** [17/30]: Storevine Reviews

**App card subtitle** (no keyword stuffing, no superlatives or stats):
Collect honest product reviews with photos, verified by real orders

**Introduction** [89/100]:
Collect photo reviews, ask buyers after delivery, and add star ratings for Google search.

**App details** [470/500]:
Storevine Reviews adds a review widget and star ratings to your product pages. After an order is fulfilled, buyers get a short, neutral email asking for a review; reviews written from it are marked Verified buyer. Shoppers can add up to 3 photos. Approve reviews before they appear or publish right away, and reply to any review. Ratings are added as Google rich-results markup and to Shopify's standard rating fields. Import from your old app and export a CSV any time.

**Features** [each ≤80]:
1. Review widget and star ratings from the theme editor
2. Review-request emails after fulfillment, with one-click opt-out
3. Verified buyer badge for reviews written from a real order
4. Up to 3 photos per review
5. Approve, hide or reply to reviews
6. Rich-results markup for Google and Shopify's standard rating fields
7. Unlimited imports and CSV export on every plan
