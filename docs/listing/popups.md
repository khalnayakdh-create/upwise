# App Store listing draft — Storevine Pop-ups

Limits from Shopify's listing requirements (checked 2026-09-30). Counts in brackets. Prices approved 2026-10-03.

**App name** [17/30]: Storevine Pop-ups

**App card subtitle** (no keyword stuffing, no superlatives or stats):
Grow your email list with a pop-up that keeps bots out

**Introduction** [96/100]:
An email sign-up pop-up that adds real subscribers to your Shopify customers and keeps bots out.

**App details** [472/500]:
Storevine Pop-ups shows a simple, accessible sign-up pop-up after a delay, on scroll or when a visitor is about to leave. Sign-ups are saved straight to your Shopify customers as email subscribers, with a record of what each person agreed to. A built-in bot shield stops fake sign-ups before they reach your list, and your dashboard shows how many were blocked. Signed-in customers can be skipped. Show a discount code after sign-up, or a unique single-use code on Growth.

**Features** [each ≤80]:
1. Sign-up pop-up after a delay, on scroll or on exit intent
2. Sign-ups saved to Shopify customers as email subscribers
3. Bot shield: rate limits, trap field, throwaway emails blocked
4. Consent record saved on each customer
5. Skip signed-in customers, or show only to those not subscribed
6. Unique single-use discount code per sign-up (Growth)
7. Keyboard and screen-reader friendly

**Search terms** [≤5, complete words, one idea each]:
- email popup
- newsletter popup
- exit intent popup
- discount popup
- email capture

**Pricing**:
- Free: unlimited sign-ups, all triggers, bot shield, consent record, one shared discount code, Storevine branding
- Growth $9.99 / 30 days, 7-day free trial: unique single-use codes, no branding. Flat price, never based on views or contacts.
